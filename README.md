# Chemboy Photography site

Open `index.html` in a browser to view the one-page site. It has no build step. The moon/sun control switches between dark and light themes and remembers the choice when browser storage is available. Click a gallery frame to open the photo viewer.

The logo crop in `assets/chemboy-mark.png` comes from the supplied Instagram screenshot. The hero film in `assets/chemboy-hero.mp4` is the supplied video; it plays muted by default and the on-video sound button enables audio at 22% volume. The gallery uses the supplied wedding and portrait photographs in `assets/gallery-*.jpg`; its buttons filter the collection by category, and gallery images open in the photo viewer. The service section includes weddings, Koito ceremonies, birthdays and celebrations, and studio or location portrait sessions. Booking buttons point to `@chemboy_photography` because no phone number or email address was supplied.
